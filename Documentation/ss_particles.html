Particle System

Particles are lightweight layer items emitted by SS_Emitter subclasses. They are best used for fx like fire, smoke, and sparks.

Particle classes

  • SS_Emitter / SS_Sprayer / SS_Dropper / SS_Streamer / SS_Fantail / SS_TriPoly

Notes

  • SS_Polygon, SS_Streamer, SS_Fantail, SS_TriPoly are extended LayerItems with strip/point-list interfaces.
